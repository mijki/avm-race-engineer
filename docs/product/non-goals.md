# Non-Goals

Status: `Planned`

The following are deliberate non-goals for the first release shape of AVM Race
Engineer.

## Product Non-Goals

- Do not turn AVM PitWall into a dense dashboard that competes with driving
- Do not require the engineer to navigate deep tab structures for routine race
  operation
- Do not treat the Relay Server as a place for accidental domain sprawl
- Do not copy V1 implementation wholesale into this repository
- Do not add strategy automation before command, telemetry, and alert trust are
  proven

## Process Non-Goals

- Do not rename major product surfaces casually
- Do not label planned or proposed behavior as shipped
- Do not introduce setup flows that hide failure behind optimistic defaults
